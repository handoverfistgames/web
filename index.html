A little help?

What should I include in a bug report?

Include the game name, your device or platform, the game version, what happened and the steps to repeat it. Screenshots or a short recording can help too. Never include passwords or payment details.

Where can I find release dates?

Confirmed game announcements and release details will be added to the Games section when available.

Can I share feedback or an idea?

Yes. Choose “Feedback or idea” in the message form and tell us what you think. Avoid sharing confidential material.

For help and feedback, email help.handoverfist@gmail.com, or prepare a message using this form.
